Process manuals

Build a chip, one step at a time

Each manual takes one process step by step, like a building-brick instruction book: a picture per step with the new part outlined, a parts list and a check. Switch to Engineer or Process engineer for the operation specification behind each step, cited to public sources.

All manuals

  • MANUAL 4B · 8 STEPS Make the photomask Mask making: blank, write, develop, etch the chrome, strip, measure, inspect and repair, mount the pellicle
  • MANUAL 4A · 10 STEPS Print a pattern with light Photolithography: prime, coat, bake, align, expose, develop, inspect
  • MANUAL 5 · 9 STEPS Carve the pattern into the film Plasma (reactive ion) etch: load, pump down, flow gas, strike plasma, etch, endpoint, over-etch, strip, measure
  • MANUAL 8 · 7 STEPS Make the surface flat again Planarization: spin-on glass in older processes, chemical mechanical polishing (CMP) today

Safety

Every step carries a safety tier. Manuals explain how a cleanroom works; they are never home procedures, and the chemistry of Tier 3 steps is never given as recipes.

  • Tier 0 safe to describe
  • Tier 1 describe with care
  • Tier 2 heat, vacuum or high-voltage equipment: no home procedure
  • Tier 3 hazardous chemistry or gases: explained conceptually only
