Process manuals

Build a chip, one step at a time

Each manual takes one process step by step, like a building-brick instruction book: a picture per step with the new part outlined, a parts list and a check. Switch to Engineer or Process engineer for the operation specification behind each step, cited to public sources.

All manuals

  • MANUAL 1 · 6 STEPS Grow the crystal and cut the wafer Czochralski growth: melt, seed, neck, body, grind, slice, polish, sort
  • MANUAL 2 · 6 STEPS Clean the wafer before every furnace Wafer cleaning, explained conceptually: count, remove films, oxide, particles and metals, rinse and count again
  • MANUAL 3 · 6 STEPS Grow glass on the silicon Thermal oxidation: load, ramp, oxidise dry or wet, anneal, unload, measure
  • MANUAL 4B · 8 STEPS Make the photomask Mask making: blank, write, develop, etch the chrome, strip, measure, inspect and repair, mount the pellicle
  • MANUAL 4A · 10 STEPS Print a pattern with light Photolithography: prime, coat, bake, align, expose, develop, inspect
  • MANUAL 5 · 9 STEPS Carve the pattern into the film Plasma (reactive ion) etch: load, pump down, flow gas, strike plasma, etch, endpoint, over-etch, strip, measure
  • MANUAL 6A · 6 STEPS Bake the dopant into the silicon Furnace diffusion: open windows, predeposit, remove the source, drive in, measure
  • MANUAL 6B · 6 STEPS Fire dopant atoms into the silicon Ion implantation: mask, tilt, implant, see the damage, anneal, measure
  • MANUAL 7A · 7 STEPS Wire it up with aluminium Metallization in a teaching process: open contacts, evaporate aluminium, measure, pattern, etch, sinter
  • MANUAL 7B · 6 STEPS Coat every surface evenly Thin-film deposition: chemical vapour deposition (CVD) and atomic layer deposition (ALD)
  • MANUAL 8 · 7 STEPS Make the surface flat again Planarization: spin-on glass in older processes, chemical mechanical polishing (CMP) today
  • MANUAL 9 · 6 STEPS Build the transistor in three dimensions Device modules: isolation, high-k metal gate, fins, a silicon–germanium stack, release and gate wrap
  • MANUAL 10 · 6 STEPS Wire the chip in copper Dual damascene: insulator, trench, barrier, seed, plate, polish, measure
  • MANUAL 11 · 7 STEPS Find the good dies and package them Back end: probe test structures, test every die, map, saw, pick, bond and test again
  • MANUAL 12 · 6 STEPS Make a transistor on glass An oxide thin-film transistor (IGZO): another process family, built at low temperature with no crystal at all
  • MANUAL 13 · 6 STEPS Make a planar transistor the 1959 way Hoerni’s planar process: grow oxide, open a window, predeposit, drive in under the oxide, evaporate leads, test breakdown

Safety

Every step carries a safety tier. Manuals explain how a cleanroom works; they are never home procedures, and the chemistry of Tier 3 steps is never given as recipes.

  • Tier 0 safe to describe
  • Tier 1 describe with care
  • Tier 2 heat, vacuum or high-voltage equipment: no home procedure
  • Tier 3 hazardous chemistry or gases: explained conceptually only
